1. Methodology & Approach

Core Principles

  • PROSCI / ADKAR for individual adoption.
  • 4P Framework for communication (Purpose, Picture, Plan, Part).
  • Agile Delivery: Iterations, RICE prioritization.

Day 1 Requirements

  • Clear Problem Statement (1-3 sentences).
  • Active Executive Sponsor (not just a title).
  • "As-Is" Process Map (if exists).

2. AI Implementation Inputs

Data & Access

  • Repositories: SharePoint, Jira, Knowledge Base.
  • Policies: GDPR, Data Retention, Access Control.
  • Use Cases: 3-5 high value targets.

Risks & Compliance

  • Hallucination Checks: Grounding & SME Review.
  • Audit Trail: Logging all AI decisions.
  • Human-in-the-loop for critical paths.
